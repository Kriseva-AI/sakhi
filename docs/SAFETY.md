# Safeguarding boundary

Sakhi is an internal-preview information navigator for adults in India. It is not an emergency service, counsellor, legal representative, medical adviser or government system. For immediate danger in India, the public 112 pathway is always visible before retrieval. No automatic call or dispatch exists. Phone reachability is not observed.

Explicit immediate-danger selections and a finite draft phrase list bypass retrieval. Unknown crisis phrasing can be missed. Never rely on a classifier to decide whether an emergency deserves help. Minors indicated by the age choice or supported text patterns stop adult flows and receive the source-backed Child Helpline entry and emergency alternative. Unsupported age phrasing is a known limitation.

Users may skip disclosure and explore general public resources. No workflow asks for intimate imagery, detailed victim narratives, suspect identities or precise addresses. The app never infers guilt, investigates people, recommends confrontation, provides personalized escape planning or guarantees outcomes. A request for unsupported advice receives abstention and general pathways.

Helping-a-friend mode supplies public discovery without entering the friend's details. Expert bystander training is withheld until reviewed. Every language, including English safeguarding text, requires domain and language review before public release. All locale stamps remain review_pending.

External institutions have their own eligibility and authentication rules. Sakhi never bypasses OTP/CAPTCHA or submits complaints. No referral, appointment, acknowledgement or live-provider availability state is simulated. All partner adapters are disabled. Trained partners, written agreements, security review and per-action consent are prerequisites for T2.
