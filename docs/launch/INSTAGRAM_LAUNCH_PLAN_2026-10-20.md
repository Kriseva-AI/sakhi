# Sakhi Instagram launch plan

2026-10-20. Status: planned creative/release package, **not a produced reel, product launch or published post**. Runtime remains the existing internal preview. The new media/AAC/23-language capabilities in the design are not yet implemented. No Instagram account, draft upload, voice session, music licence, paid promotion or public URL was used/created.

[Product design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-sakhi-care-multimodal-design.md). [Execution plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-20-sakhi-care-multimodal.md). [Primary-source research](../research/multimodal-care-2026-10-20.md).

## Launch sequence

1. Build and review the care/AAC/localisation slice using fictional data. Recruit consented reviewers through a separately authorised route; never ask people to post private stories or intimate media in comments/DMs.
2. Complete media/model/private-processing gates and the advertised language/mode matrix. No animation of an unfinished feature can substitute for it working.
3. Accept the hosted release, actual source/contact and capacity evidence, real incident/disclosure/moderation owners, privacy/child/media/security and accessibility/device gates. Register rollback/disable-media procedures.
4. Create the final reel from that exact accepted build, review its claims/rights/accessibility and test the actual public CTA and platform draft. A public launch needs a real working HTTPS destination. No localhost or invented Sakhi domain in release creative.
5. Publish only the reviewed package after a concrete release/publish decision. Observe actual success, failure and rollback. A scheduled/drafted/uploaded item is not a published post; publication is not proof of adoption or safety impact.

The [owner/gate register](../evidence/multimodal-planning-2026-10-20/launch-gates.csv) names required roles and current gaps without inventing staffed teams.

No launch date is promised until owners, review capacity and dependencies are real. Public-interest intent does not establish charity registration or permission to use restricted assets/models.

## Creative direction

Lead with agency and practical help. Bold pink/black, clear human typography, calm pacing and actual interface. One idea per frame, no flashing alarms, attack reenactments, fear statistics or promises of prevention. Do not borrow women's organisations' logos or imply endorsement. Never use participant/survivor material as promotional footage merely because they consented to a study.

Target production format, not a claim of platform minimum requirements:30seconds,9:16,1080x1920,30fps,H.264 video/AAC audio; captioned master, clean master, SRT/descriptive transcript and cover. Source font/icon/music/voice rights retained with the package. Verify the actual Instagram draft crop/overlays before release because a direct help-page fetch was rate limited during research.

## Acceptance and launch-day operations

- Sound-off comprehension: all important information in reviewed captions; readable display time, contrast and no reliance on colour. Add descriptive transcript with the live link; inspect with screen reader/Braille text access. Provide the ISL version when its review gate passes.
- Actual phones: preview vertical crop and platform UI overlays; no obscured consent/status text or real recipient/device position. Caption timing and voice are inspected by native speakers.
- Hosted journey: static emergency action before bundles/API; test every advertised language/mode and denied/failure/offline alternatives. Confirm no hidden private upload, analytics or model download. Test media-disabled and provider-outage rollback.
- Check predeclared capacity limits before publishing. Start with a controllable release and narrative-free technical monitoring; named on-call person can disable media or pause promotion. Planned metrics are not achieved service levels.
- Comment moderation/disclosure: public safety scope notice, no requests for private stories, real designated owner and defined handling for accidental disclosures. Do not invite clinical/emergency reliance through social replies.
- Do not infer success from views. Track only consented nonsensitive feedback and aggregate technical health; never add narrative/session replay to measure conversion. Any critical privacy/consent/emergency regression stops promotion pending correction.
