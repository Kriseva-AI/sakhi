## Care and accessibility evidence

- [WHO clinical handbook](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-14.26) and [LIVES training](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240039803/) address listening, validation, practical needs and support. Their intended audience includes trained health providers. Sakhi can adopt nonjudgmental communication and choice; it cannot claim to provide their clinical intervention or substitute a professional.
- [ASHA AAC practice portal](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/professional-issues/augmentative-and-alternative-communication/) describes picture/text boards, speech-generating tools and alternate access. Select a symbol-and-text communication board as the additional input mode, with undo/confirm and no mandatory speech. It is not a diagnostic tool or an assumption about every disability.
- [W3C input modalities](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/input-modalities.html) and [pointer gestures](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pointer-gestures) support concurrent input methods and alternatives to complex gestures. Use semantic controls that work with OS switches/eye-gaze, not camera-based gaze tracking in Sakhi. Physical assistive-device testing remains necessary.
- [W3C sign languages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/sign-languages/) makes clear that sign language and written captions serve different needs. Build Deaf-led ISL access, captions and descriptive transcripts; do not equate a dictionary with conversational recognition.
- [ISLRTC FAQ](https://islrtc.nic.in/faq/) describes a10,000-term video dictionary and attribution/non-profiteering conditions. Its homepage currently lists a video relay service and limited weekday hours. Treat this as a candidate independently accessed alternative after scope/contact review, not an integrated emergency service. Do not promise availability or import a video corpus without rights/accessibility review. [ISLRTC homepage](https://islrtc.nic.in/)
- [W3C media planning](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/planning/) informs captions, descriptive transcript and access without sound. Public guidance and the reel need these from scripting onwards, not as a last export step.
- [NNEDV documentation considerations](https://www.techsafety.org/documentationtips) and [messaging-evidence guidance](https://www.techsafety.org/messaging-evidence) explain that documentation has safety/context limits. Preserve originals under the person's control, label interpretations as derivatives and avoid evidence custody, recording advice or admissibility guarantees.

## Browser and transport constraints

[MDN SpeechRecognition](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SpeechRecognition) warns that some implementations send audio to a remote engine and are not universally supported. [On-device recognition](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Speech_API/Using_the_Web_Speech_API) requires capability/language-pack checks. Never silently use a browser speech service as if it were offline/private.

[MediaRecorder.isTypeSupported](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaRecorder/isTypeSupported_static) is a capability check, not a guarantee against resource failure. [getUserMedia](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaDevices/getUserMedia) requires secure contexts and device permission. Plan explicit record/review/interpret actions, MIME negotiation, stopped tracks and unsupported-codec recovery. Microphone consent is not server-processing or sharing consent.

[MDN speech-voice localService](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SpeechSynthesisVoice/localService) distinguishes local from remote synthesis. Prefer reviewed prerecorded public guidance and available local voices, with sound off by default. Do not read a private narrative through a remote voice without a separate informed choice.

## Legal and launch dependencies

The rules have phased commencement. Do not treat every disability as lack of capacity or force disclosure to a possibly abusive caregiver. No legal compliance conclusion is given by this plan.

## Recommendation and alternatives

1. Recommended: improve care/AAC and complete whole-product localisation first; add consent-separated media interpretation on owned, bounded infrastructure; keep public/offline core usable when that service is absent. Deliver all requested input modes with explicit per-language capability states and honest fallbacks.
2. Cloud-first media/chat would reduce some integration work but adds private-data, provider, retention, cost and licence dependencies. Do not choose it without provider-specific informed consent and deployment review.
3. A native-first full rebuild could improve some device/background integrations, but adds platform/accessibility/operations scope and still cannot create institutional authority or guaranteed rescue. Keep the PWA; evaluate a native companion only after real background requirements and staffing exist.
