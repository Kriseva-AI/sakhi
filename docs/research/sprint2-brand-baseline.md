# Sprint 2: verified local brand and product baseline

Date: 2026-10-19. Status: inspection evidence, before redesign.

The founder rejected Sprint 1's visual minimalism as too basic. The new brief requires minimal but information-rich, friendly, illustrative and highly interactive UI; prevention plus post-concern support; clear privacy; visible official pathways; free public-benefit intent; ten or more meaningful differentiators; researched maps and messaging feasibility.

Current verified implementation: 35 Python, 6 browser-local and 74 browser tests; 410 draft scenario fixtures; 7 curated official public entry points; no institutional integrations. Native and Docker work locally. Lighthouse mobile simulation scored 99/100/100 after compression. All locale reviews and safeguarding approval are pending. These are engineering observations, not real-world safety outcomes.

Canonical brand inspected:

- 02_PUBLIC_PRESENCE/corporate_website/website/src/styles/tokens-v6.css: current shared corporate tokens, describing Evidential Cartography. Warm paper, deep navy, scarce brass, evidence at the point of use, visible boundaries, no generic gradient aesthetic.
- 02_PUBLIC_PRESENCE/corporate_website/website/public/fonts/fonts.css: self-hosted Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, with versioned Fontsource provenance. No third-party font requests.
- 01_PRODUCTS/tas/KRISEVA_TAS_DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md: foundation and token sections inspected as brand provenance. TAS is a procurement operator surface; its dense institutional workflow is not transplanted into a women-facing public support product.
- 00_BRAIN/FACTS.md: public claims remain evidence-bounded. A free public-benefit project is not represented as a legally registered nonprofit or government-affiliated service.

Selected direction: human public infrastructure, using the corporate navy/paper/brass and font contract, with larger readable copy, fewer decorative symbols, explicit public-pathway labels and interactive explanation. Instrument Serif for short display text; IBM Plex Sans for all actions and reading; Mono only for source dates, status and technical identifiers. Native Indic fallback, RTL and review notices remain visible.

Primary safeguarding references inspected:

- [NNEDV Safety Net survivor resources](https://www.techsafety.org/resources-survivors): specialist technology/privacy resources, distinct from emergency dispatch. US service numbers are not imported into Indian user routes.
- [WHO clinical handbook](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-14.26): distinguishes immediate psychological/physical needs, ongoing safety and ongoing support. Its clinical guidance is for providers and is not converted into an AI clinician.
- [FTC stalkerware guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/stalkerware-what-know): highlights device-monitoring concerns. Sakhi must not casually prescribe setting changes or claim stealth.
- [Official cybercrime safety material](https://cybercrime.gov.in/webform/Crime_OnlineSafetyTips.aspx): supports preventive digital-awareness resource discovery without requiring a report.
- [Right To Be bystander methodology](https://righttobe.org/guides/bystander-intervention-training/): specialist training reference. Personalized intervention advice still requires qualified review; no instruction to confront someone is generated.

Research synthesis and the implementation plan will distinguish what is source observed, a design inference, functional now, or authorization gated. No private data, exact-location collection, live dispatch, safe-route guarantee or automated WhatsApp authority connection is introduced by design language alone.
