# Community conduct

Respect people's privacy, choices and dignity. Do not pressure anyone to disclose, report, provide evidence or continue a discussion. Harassment, threats, victim blaming, discriminatory language, sexualized material and doxxing are prohibited.

Do not use public issues for crisis support or private case details. Maintainers may remove sensitive content and restrict unsafe contributions. Government and third-party resource links do not imply partnership. Moderation and safety roles must be assigned before public community launch.

For immediate danger in India, use the official 112 pathway. This repository is not monitored as an emergency channel.
