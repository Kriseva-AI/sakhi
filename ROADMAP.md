# Roadmap and honest scope

Current scope: internal local preview, including the founder-requested public-only Sprint 2 extensions. The original 68-feature priorities remain in FEATURES.json; overlapping partial prototypes are labelled. Private workflows and institutional P1/P2 adapters remain disabled. The fourteen Sprint 2 interactions are described in docs/SPRINT2_FEATURES.md. No date, partnership or measured user benefit is promised.

## Gate 1: a credible public navigator

- Independently verify critical contacts, jurisdiction, hours and current source scope through appropriate authorized channels. Do not make test emergency calls or send fictional cases to providers.
- Obtain safeguarding and domain review of every exposed pathway and learning card. Resolve F03, F07 and F48 with actual signed/versioned review evidence. A functional directory is not a provider network.
- Commission native-language and script review. Launch only the locale/content combinations actually approved; keep reviewed English fallback explicit. Do not turn structural keys or font glyph coverage into a language-quality claim.
- Establish a held-out multilingual evaluation set with qualified labels and independent adjudication. Include uncertainty, romanized phrases, non-age numbers, minors and emergencies. Report failures by language and category, not a single synthetic score.
- Run moderated usability with fictional scenarios, including readers with low digital familiarity and users of assistive technology. Do not solicit real personal cases for product testing. Validate discoverability, comprehension, error recovery and decision control, not only task speed.
- Perform physical Android/iOS, screen-reader, browser zoom, reduced-motion, slow-network and cold offline checks. Browser offline emulation is insufficient for the physical gate.
- Review production ingress, HTTPS, rate limits, logging, dependencies, license redistribution, incident response and an actual private security channel. Verify deployment rather than treating CI configuration as a successful run.

Pass condition: all applicable gates recorded with evidence, owners and dates. Choose an accurately scoped public preview, not a claim of nationwide emergency reliability. No public GitHub remote, remote Issues, tag, hosted crisis service or CI run exists today.

## Gate 2: service discovery that deserves trust

The current Google Maps handoff is a public search across sixteen preset cities. It needs no API key but cannot certify a provider, opening hours, appointment or safe route. A real district directory needs individual official source records, update responsibility, contact checks, service eligibility and language/accessibility information. Expand sourced records before claiming coverage. Optional Maps APIs need an actual account, current terms, billing controls and reviewed key restrictions; free quotas are not assumed.

Historical NCRB/OGD aggregates can support documented research questions after provenance, time period, geography, definitions, completeness and licensing are established. No raw nationwide dataset was acquired in Sprint 2. Registered-case counts are not live danger, a probability of victimization, alleged-offender intelligence or a route-safety signal. The support journey must remain usable without them.

## Gate 3: an actual human connection

A staffed help channel requires a real authorized operator, trained safeguarding staff, defined hours, response expectations, escalation ownership and a tested fallback. A WhatsApp draft is not such a channel. WhatsApp Business deployment additionally requires policy eligibility, opt-in, account/API approval, appropriate templates and secure verified webhooks. Current restrictions involving law-enforcement use need specific review; a citizen opening a public contact is a different action. No recipient or integration is invented.

Location-triggered authority alerts require a separately approved adapter and written authority/provider agreements. Design explicit purpose-specific permission, coarse versus precise location choice, exact recipient/payload preview, minimal retention, revocation and device/background limits. Browser location permission alone authorizes neither transmission nor dispatch. Model-independent 112 access remains available. Test only in an authorized sandbox until a provider approves real acceptance testing.

Required states: draft, reviewed, approved, send attempted, sent, delivered and provider accepted are distinct. Each state needs real observable evidence from an authorized adapter. A timeout or click never becomes an acknowledgement. No current interface may show these operations as live.

## Gate 4: private workflows

User-controlled summaries, reference tracking, evidence metadata, personal reminders and encrypted storage require a separate threat model, legal/safeguarding review, deletion/export design and security audit. No intimate media uploads, suspect scoring or AI-generated personalized escape, medical or legal instructions are introduced through a visual redesign. Open-source contribution does not bypass review of critical public information.
